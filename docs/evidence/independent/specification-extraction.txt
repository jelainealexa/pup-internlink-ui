Important UX decision: Requirements should have two connected views: the master list of
required documents and the student submission review list. This avoids mixing document
configuration with individual student submissions

The system should also identify students with incomplete mandatory requirements because the
ERD specifies that all mandatory requirements must be submitted before a student becomes
eligible for placement
