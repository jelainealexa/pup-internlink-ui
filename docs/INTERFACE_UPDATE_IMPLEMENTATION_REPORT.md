## Five-role permissions and assumptions

UI guards and service-operation guards both enforce scope. Company administrators cannot decide applications, review student documents or approve progress/evaluate. Interns cannot administer companies/accounts or access unassigned opportunities/interns/unrelated document phases. Students see own records plus eligible Active postings/permitted company context. Coordinators administer all records but cannot skip approval/eligibility/duplicate/capacity rules.
